## 🇬🇧 English Description

Alisizm No Prop Damage ensures a more stable and controlled gameplay experience by reducing unnecessary chaos caused by players. It prevents the destruction of world props, eliminating common trolling methods and excessive network load caused by prop syncing after explosions.

### 🔧 Features
*   🚗 **Gas Stations:** Do not explode when hit by vehicles.
*   🚧 **Poles:** Street lights and utility poles do not break.
*   🪧 **Signs:** Traffic and street signs remain indestructible.
*   🚒 **Fire Hydrants:** Cannot be destroyed (prevents water geysers).
*   ⚡ **General Props:** Protects various world/environment props from physical damage.
*   Puzzle **Compatibility:** Works out-of-the-box with **QBCore, ESX, and Qbox**.
*   ⚙️ **Optimized:** Built with sync-friendly logic and very low CPU usage (0.00ms idle).
